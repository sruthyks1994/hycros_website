Pricing questions

What does the free trial include?

Everything in Pro, for 7 days, for your whole team. No card is asked for.

How do I pay?

Inside HYCROS, on the Subscription page. Choose a plan and pay by card through Stripe. Your invoices are kept in your account.

Is yearly billing cheaper?

Yes. {{ANNUAL_NOTE}} compared with paying month by month.

How is a user counted?

Plans are per user. Add and remove people whenever your team changes.

Can I cancel, or get my money back?

You can cancel at any time, with no lock-in. HYCROS also comes with a money-back guarantee: if it is not right for you after you subscribe, ask and we will refund you.

What if I do not choose a plan after the trial?

On day 7 the workspace is suspended. Your data is kept, so you can pick a plan later and carry on where you left off.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Your site admin can download your organisation's data as a ZIP of CSV files whenever you need it, including people, projects, tasks, timesheets, leave and the audit log.

Do you help us get started?

Yes. Every new customer gets a free onboarding call to set up departments, task lifecycles and members. Book it here.
