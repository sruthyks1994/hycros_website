Pricing questions

What does the free trial include?

Everything in Pro, for 7 days, for your whole team. No card is asked for.

How do I pay?

Inside HYCROS. Choose a plan and pay securely by card. Your invoices are kept in your account.

Is yearly billing cheaper?

Yes. {{ANNUAL_NOTE}} compared with paying month by month.

How are people counted?

You pay for each person who uses HYCROS. Add and remove people whenever your team changes.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time, with no lock-in. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for and does not renew.

What if I do not choose a plan after the trial?

On day 7 the workspace is suspended. Your data is kept, so you can pick a plan later and carry on where you left off.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. You can download your organisation's data as spreadsheet files whenever you need it, including people, projects, tasks, timesheets, leave and the full history of changes.

Do you help us get started?

Yes. Every new customer gets a free onboarding call to set up departments, your way of working and your team. Book it here.
