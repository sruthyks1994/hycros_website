Security

Your data stays in your workspace.

Every company has its own space. Who can see what inside it is decided by roles and settings you control.

Companies are kept apart.

Every query is limited to the signed-in company. A record that belongs to another company is answered as "not found", the same as a record that does not exist. Automated checks run on every change and fail if one company could read or change another's records.

Sign-in options

Password, Google or Microsoft sign-in, or your own single sign-on (SAML). Repeated failed sign-ins are rate limited. Password rules and reset links are built in.

Provisioning from your directory

Accounts can be created and switched off automatically from your identity provider with SCIM, so leavers lose access the day they leave.

Roles and access

Admins, managers, team leads and employees see what their role allows. Leads, clients and billing are visible only to the people you give access to.

Your data after the trial

If a trial ends without a plan, the workspace is suspended and sign-in is blocked. Your data is kept, so you can pick a plan and carry on.
