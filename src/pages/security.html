Security

Your data, under your control.

You choose how people log in and who sees what. HYCROS keeps a record of every change, and your data is always yours to download.

Who gets in.

Login options

People log in with a password, with the Microsoft or Google account they already use, or with your company's own login system. You choose which options are allowed. Repeated wrong passwords are slowed down, and password rules and reset links are built in.

Joiners and leavers, handled automatically

If your IT team manages accounts centrally (for example with Microsoft), HYCROS can create, update and switch off accounts on its own, so people who leave lose access the day they go.

Unattended screens sign out

If someone walks away from their computer, HYCROS signs them out after the time you set, so nobody else can use their account.

Sharing without accounts

Clients see how their project is going through a private link. They can look but not change anything, and they never get an account or a way into the rest of your workspace.

What they see.

Roles

Admins, managers, team leads and employees each see what their role allows. A task opens only for the people who are allowed to open it, on the task page and on boards alike.

Departments and groups

Group people from any department and give the group access to a project. Everyone else only sees the projects they belong to.

Sensitive information

Sales, clients and billing are visible only to the people you give access to. Features you do not use can be switched off for everyone.

Approvals with reasons

Changes to deadlines and estimates, late edits and finished work go to the people you choose for approval, and the reason for each change is kept.

What changed, and your data.

A full history

See everything that happened on a project or task, even ones that were deleted, and who did it.

Replay the timeline

Play back what happened on a project, task or person's day between two dates, step by step.

Download your data

Download your organisation's data as spreadsheet files whenever you want: people, projects, tasks, comments, timesheets, leave, attendance and the full history of changes.

Your data after the trial

If a trial ends without a plan, the workspace is paused and nobody can log in. Your data is kept, so you can pick a plan and carry on.
