Security

Your data, under your control.

You choose how people sign in and who sees what. HYCROS keeps a record of what changed, and your data is yours to download.

Who gets in.

Sign-in options

Password, Google or Microsoft sign-in, or your own single sign-on (SAML). Your admin picks which ones are on. Repeated failed sign-ins are rate limited, and password rules and reset links are built in.

Provisioning from your directory

Microsoft Entra ID, or another identity provider, can add, update and switch off accounts automatically with SCIM, so leavers lose access the day they leave.

Sessions that end

Idle sessions sign out after the timeout you set for your organisation, so an unattended screen does not stay open.

Sharing without accounts

Clients see a read-only project status page through a private link. They never get an account or a way into the rest of your workspace.

What they see.

Roles

Admins, managers, team leads and employees each see what their role allows. A task opens only for the people who are allowed to open it, on the task page and on boards alike.

Departments and user groups

Group people from any department, then assign the group to a project so its members can see it. Everyone else in the team only sees the projects they belong to.

Sensitive modules

Leads, clients and billing are visible only to the people you give access to. Modules you do not use can be switched off for everyone.

Approvals with reasons

Due-date and estimate changes, overdue edits and completions go through the approvers you set, and the reason for each change is kept.

What changed, and your data.

Activity logs

Search the full history of a project or task, including deleted ones, and browse audited actions across the workspace.

Timeline replay

Replay what was recorded for a project, task or person between two dates, step by step.

Export your data

Your site admin can download your organisation's data as a ZIP of CSV files: people, projects, tasks, comments, timesheets, leave, attendance and the audit log.

Your data after the trial

If a trial ends without a plan, the workspace is suspended and sign-in is blocked. Your data is kept, so you can pick a plan and carry on.
