Questions

Do I need a card to start?

No. Sign up with your work email, confirm it and your workspace is ready.

What happens after 7 days?

The workspace is suspended and nobody can sign in until you choose a plan or contact us. Your data is kept.

Where is my data, and who can see it?

In your own company workspace. Other companies cannot see or change it, and every sign-in is checked against your company.

Can I set it up for my kind of team?

Pick a starter pack at sign-up (general office, software team or e-learning), or start empty and set up your own departments.

How do people sign in?

With a password, Google or Microsoft, or your own single sign-on (SAML). Accounts can also be provisioned automatically from your directory (SCIM).

Why not just use one tool per job?

You can. But then tasks, hours, leads and leave live in different places, and nobody can see the whole picture. HYCROS keeps it in one place and switches on only the parts you use.
