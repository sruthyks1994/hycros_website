Questions

Do I need a card to start?

No. Sign up with your work email, confirm it and your workspace is ready.

How do I pay when the trial ends?

Choose Starter or Pro inside HYCROS and pay securely by card. Your invoices are kept in your account. Everything your team set up during the trial stays as it is.

What happens if I do not choose a plan?

After 7 days the workspace is suspended and nobody can sign in until you choose a plan or contact us. Your data is kept.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel at any time, with no lock-in: your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for and does not renew. Before you leave, you can download all of your data as spreadsheet files.

Is yearly billing cheaper?

Yes. {{ANNUAL_NOTE}} compared with paying month by month.

Will someone help us set it up?

Yes. Every new customer gets a free onboarding call to set up departments, your way of working and your team. Book it here.

Who can see our data?

Only the people you invite, and each person sees only what their role allows. Sales, clients and billing are visible only to the people you give access to.

How do people log in?

With a password, with the Microsoft or Google account they already use, or with your company's own login system. If your IT team manages accounts centrally, people can also be added and removed automatically.

Do my team need to be technical?

No. HYCROS is built for everyone in the organisation, not just IT. Most people learn it in an afternoon, and the free onboarding call gets you set up.

Why not just use one tool per job?

You can. But then tasks, hours, sales and leave live in different places, you pay for each, and nobody can see the whole picture. HYCROS keeps it all in one place and switches on only the parts you use.
