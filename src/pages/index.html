Questions

Do I need a card to start?

No. Sign up with your work email, confirm it and your workspace is ready.

How do I pay when the trial ends?

Choose Starter or Pro on the Subscription page inside HYCROS and pay by card through Stripe. Your invoices are kept in your account. Everything your team set up during the trial stays as it is.

What happens if I do not choose a plan?

After 7 days the workspace is suspended and nobody can sign in until you choose a plan or contact us. Your data is kept.

Can I cancel, or get my money back?

Yes. You can cancel at any time, with no lock-in, and HYCROS comes with a money-back guarantee if it is not right for you. Before you leave, your site admin can download all of your data as CSV files.

Is yearly billing cheaper?

Yes. {{ANNUAL_NOTE}} compared with paying month by month.

Will someone help us set it up?

Yes. Every new customer gets a free onboarding call to set up departments, task lifecycles and members. Book it here.

Who can see our data?

Only the people you invite, and each person sees only what their role allows. Leads, clients and billing are visible only to the people you give access to.

How do people sign in?

With a password, Google or Microsoft, or your own single sign-on (SAML). Accounts can also be provisioned automatically from your directory (SCIM).

Why not just use one tool per job?

You can. But then tasks, hours, leads and leave live in different places, you pay for each, and nobody can see the whole picture. HYCROS keeps it in one place and switches on only the parts you use.
