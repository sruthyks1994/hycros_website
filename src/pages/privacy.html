The short version

  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • The data your team puts into HYCROS belongs to your organisation. We process it only to run the service for you.
  • Our support team can see inside your workspace only when your Site Admin gives permission. That permission expires by itself, and every look is logged.
  • You can download your organisation's data at any time.

2. Your organisation's data and ours

There are two kinds of personal data, and responsibility for each is different.

  • Workspace data is everything your organisation and its people put into HYCROS. Your organisation decides what goes in, who can see it and how long it is kept. It is the data fiduciary (or controller), and we process the data on its behalf. If you are an employee with a question about your workspace data, please ask your organisation's HYCROS Admin first. We will help them answer.
  • Account data is what we need to run our business: the sign-up details, billing records, support conversations and visits to this website. We are responsible for it.

3. What we collect

When you visit this website

  • Our web server records standard request logs: IP address, browser type, the page asked for and the time. We use them to keep the site running and secure.
  • The website's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads.
  • The contact form opens your own email app. We receive only what you choose to send.

When you sign up

  • Your organisation's name, your name and your work email address.
  • The version of our terms and this policy you accepted, and when you accepted them.
  • A check from Cloudflare Turnstile that you are a person and not a bot. To do this, Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser information.
  • To stop abuse, we keep a scrambled (one-way hashed) form of your IP address and email address. We delete it after 2 days.
  • When your workspace is created, we record the IP address and browser it was created from in our audit log.

Inside the product (workspace data)

What is collected depends on the features your organisation uses. It can include:

  • People: name, work email, department, job title, manager, date of joining and role. If your organisation signs in with Microsoft, some of these can come from Microsoft.
  • Work: tasks, projects, comments, checklists, attachments, leads, clients and follow-ups.
  • Time and attendance: timers, timesheets and statuses such as working, in a meeting or on a break; leave and work-from-home requests; and, if your organisation connects eTimeOffice, check-in and check-out times. HYCROS does not record punches itself. Your organisation's authorised people can review this activity, for example in timeline replay.
  • Preferences: time zone, notification choices and wellness reminder settings. Some preferences, such as saved views, are kept only in your browser.
  • Security records: sign-in attempts, sessions, and an audit log of changes made in the workspace.

When you pay

  • Your plan, number of users, invoices, billing address and GST number, if you give us one.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

When you contact support

Your messages to us and the details you share in them.

4. Why we use it

  • To provide HYCROS: to create and run your workspace and do what your organisation asks of it. We need this to perform our contract with your organisation.
  • To send service emails: confirming your sign-up, trial reminders, the trial-ended notice, invoices, password resets and the notifications each person chooses in their profile.
  • To keep the service secure: to block bots, stop abuse and investigate problems.
  • To bill and meet legal duties: to charge for plans and keep tax and accounting records.
  • To improve HYCROS: using counts and statistics, not the content of your work.

We send marketing emails only to people who have agreed to receive them, and each one has an unsubscribe link. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

6. Our support team and your workspace

Our staff cannot browse your workspace. If you need help, your Site Admin can give our support team time-limited access from a support ticket. That access lasts 24 hours unless the Admin chooses otherwise, and never more than 7 days. The Admin can revoke it at any time. There are two levels:

  • Diagnostics: settings and system health only, never the content of your work.
  • View as a member: a read-only view as one named person, who sees a banner while it is happening.

Every look is logged, and your Site Admin can read that log afterwards.

7. Where your data is stored

HYCROS is hosted in {{HOSTING_LOCATION}}. Some of our service providers, such as Stripe, Cloudflare and Google, may process data in other countries. Where this happens, we rely on their contractual and security commitments to protect it.

8. How long we keep it

  • Workspace data: for as long as your organisation uses HYCROS. If a trial ends without a plan, the workspace is suspended and the data is kept so you can carry on later. Your Admin can ask us to delete it sooner.
  • After cancellation: we keep the data for 180 days, so you can come back or download it. Then we permanently delete it, including uploaded files. Copies in backups are removed as the backups expire.
  • Unfinished sign-ups: deleted within a day after the confirmation link expires.
  • Billing records: for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Web server logs: {{LOG_RETENTION}}.

9. How we protect it

  • All traffic to HYCROS is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, never as plain text. Saved integration secrets are encrypted.
  • Access inside a workspace is limited by roles, departments and user groups, and changes are recorded in an audit log.
  • Organisations can use single sign-on, automatic provisioning and session time limits.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell the affected organisations and the authorities as the law requires.

Your organisation's Admin can download the workspace data at any time from Settings → Data export. To use your rights, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL_LINK}}. For workspace data, we may pass your request to your organisation, because it decides what happens to that data. We reply within 30 days.
